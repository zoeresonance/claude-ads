# Stone Creek Church — Community Insights™
---

## Data & Methodology

We use a multifaceted approach to understand your community's nuances and gain a deep insight into its residents' demographics, psychographics, and behavior. This knowledge forms the foundation of your "Key Persona" and "One."

Our primary sources of data are industry leaders, ERSI and Kantar. Kantar is a vital part of WPP, the world's leading advertising agency. These organizations gather extensive consumer data through an exhaustive collection process that includes credit card transactions, online behaviors, retail purchases, social media engagements, media consumption patterns, surveys, mobile phone records, and the US Census. Their data scientists construct precise population profiles and accurately predict future behaviors.

We also use supplementary resources such as Gallup, Pew, PPRI, and the Association of Religion Data Archives (ARDA) to create a comprehensive composite of your community.

Our proprietary system, which is a culmination of tested best practices, decades of hands-on experience, and in-house data analysis, transforms this foundational information into your Key Persona and One.

**Demographics** are statistical data relating to the population and particular groups within. Data is grouped into two categories: Individuals and Households.

**Psychographics** is the study and classification of people according to their attitudes, aspirations, and other psychological criteria.

## Section 5: LifeMode Groups (Tapestry Segmentation)

The US population is made up of 67 "Tapestries." Tapestry Segmentation provides detailed summaries of communities across the United States. Residential areas are grouped together into smaller, more manageable segments based on shared demographic, socioeconomic, and lifestyle characteristics. Neighborhoods with the most similar characteristics are grouped together, and neighborhoods showing divergent characteristics are separated.

For a broader view of markets, segments are summarized into LifeMode groups. LifeMode groups share similar demographic characteristics and consumer behavior patterns. The 67 Tapestries are grouped in 16 Lifemodes.

## Section 6: The One & Key Persona — Framework

We have two distinct audiences. The first audience, known as your "Key Persona," is broad and encompasses both genders, with a wider age and income range. The second audience is your "One," which is a highly defined audience within your Key Persona. Our main goal is to reach your Key Persona through your One.

Your One is the most crucial person in your Key Persona, and you must have a clear understanding of this person to make decisions based on whether the ministry, event, or design is right for them. While the goal is to reach everyone, focusing on this specific individual will help you create corporate ministries, communications, branding, and marketing that will appeal to your One and, in turn, drive growth in other areas.

### Key Characteristics

**Motivations:** Desire for cultural enrichment, maintaining a comfortable and stylish home, effective financial planning for retirement.

**Anxieties:** Concerns about financial security for retirement, maintaining property value, and keeping up with technological advancements.

**Activities:** Engaging in cultural activities such as arts and travel, extensive reading, utilizing advanced technology for both leisure and practical needs.

**Technology, Appearance, Family, Status:** Highly value technology; appearance is likely sophisticated yet practical; family size is small, focusing more on quality of life; status is conveyed through lifestyle rather than overt wealth.

**Religion:** Not specifically mentioned, but they likely hold diverse or liberal views given their lifestyle preferences.

**Top-Rated Brands:** Brands that offer sophistication, reliability, and value; likely prefer technology brands that provide cutting-edge features and brands that align with their cultural tastes.

### Taste & Style

MTs are often highly educated and cultured, with a strong appreciation for craftsmanship and culture. They place great importance on their personal appearance and the style of brands they associate themselves with. Timeless and clean designs tend to appeal to these individuals, and they are often drawn to brands that exude a sense of status. This group stays at boutique luxury hotels, prefers high-end boutique coffee cafes, and dines at trendy restaurants.

### Social Media & Brand Engagement

**Social Media Usage:** Highly connected using smartphones; likely active on platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn, and Instagram, using these for news, cultural content, and social interactions.

**Engagement Strategies:** Brands should leverage digital marketing strategies focusing on quality, sophistication, and value. Engaging through educational and cultural content online will resonate well with this group.

### Marketing Recommendations

To effectively engage the "GenXurban In Style" segment, brands should:
- **Promote Technological Convenience and Innovation:** Highlight products that integrate seamlessly into a sophisticated lifestyle, offering both style and functionality.
- **Cultural and Lifestyle Alignment:** Engage them with marketing that aligns with cultural events, arts, and travel, perhaps sponsoring events or creating content that resonates with their interests.
- **Financial Products for Retirement Planning:** Offer financial services products that aid in retirement planning, focusing on security and growth.
- **Quality and Reliability:** Focus on the durability and lasting value of products.

**Who She Is**

Sarah is a cosmopolitan professional who cherishes an urbane lifestyle filled with cultural enrichment and personal growth. Typically found in older, metropolitan neighborhoods of Urbana, IL, these residents are predominantly professional couples or singles. Their lifestyle allows them to dedicate time and resources to their homes and personal interests.

**Pursuit of Cultural and Artistic Engagement**

Sarah is deeply invested in surrounding herself with culture and the arts. She actively participates in local art events, visits museums, and travels extensively to satisfy her love for new experiences and knowledge. This cultural engagement is not just a hobby but a significant part of her identity.

**Financial Security**

Sarah prioritizes financial security. She proactively plans her finances, invests wisely, and ensures that her lifestyle will be sustainable through her retirement years. Her lifestyle and choices reflect her commitment to a life filled with culture, comfort, and sophistication while maintaining a sharp focus on her future security.

**Community Involvement**

Sarah is an active member of her local community. She participates in neighborhood associations, engages in community events, and collaborates with other parents to foster a sense of belonging. Her motivation stems from a desire to create a closely-knit community where her family can thrive.

**Outdoor and Recreational Opportunities**

Sarah is an outdoor enthusiast who understands the importance of recreational opportunities. She encourages her children to explore the local parks and playgrounds and engage in leisure activities, contributing to their well-rounded development. Her motivation is to provide her children with a variety of experiences.

**Family Time and Work-Life Balance**

She is a hardworking mother who is often overwhelmed by her responsibilities, leaving her feeling drained, inadequate, and isolated. While her marriage is stable, she and her husband are beginning to grow apart due to a lack of quality time and financial strain. These challenges only add to her already mounting anxieties. Her lifestyle leaves little time for personal care (health, spirituality, rest, etc.). As a result, friendships suffer, and many longtime friends drift away. In addition, recent data informs us that loneliness is now an epidemic punishing their mental and emotional well-being. All of this exasperates an already high state of anxiety.

**Faith**

From time to time, Sarah considers trying a church. But, if she were to attend one, it would have to be different than her past experiences. While her childhood church wasn't bad, it felt dull and disconnected from real life.

Additional roadblocks to attending church include politics, her spouse, and her kids. She doesn't want to align with the hard-right Christians she sees on TV and social media. She's also open-minded on cultural issues and doesn't like the perceived hate she sees from people claiming to be Christian.

Sarah may also feel uncomfortable discussing church with her husband, as he is less likely to be interested. Also, her kids are not expected to be enthusiastic about attending church for the first time.

Today, her spiritual practice focuses on famous authors and media self-help icons like Brené Brown, Elizabeth Gilbert, and Glennon Doyle. Business leaders like Simon Sinek, Adam M. Grant, and Timothy Ferriss also provide life lessons.

**Faith Beliefs**

Sarah's upbringing and current lifestyle suggest that while traditional religious practices may not be a central focus, she likely values spiritual growth and cultural diversity. Her extensive involvement in the arts and travel indicates an openness to exploring a variety of philosophical and cultural perspectives, which could include spiritual or non-traditional expressions of faith. ### Messaging — Least Likely to Respond To

When communicating with Sarah, being mindful of the language used in your messages is essential. Avoid making her feel she needs to do more or is not doing enough. Such messaging can create stress and make her feel inadequate. It is also vital to avoid messaging questioning her parenting abilities, as this topic can be sensitive and personal. Negative or judgmental tones should also be avoided, as they can be off-putting to Sarah. You should ensure that your messaging does not vilify or talk down to others.

Messages that feel bland or similar to what Sarah grew up with may not resonate with her. Instead, we should focus on positive outcomes and the personal significance of faith in everyday language that resonates with her motivations and fears. It is equally important to avoid clichés or messages that assume she shares the same beliefs.

Finally, we should avoid overloading Sarah with more responsibilities. She may already feel overwhelmed by the demands of her life, and messages requiring her to take on more may not be well-received.
